The problem

Manual measure-ups
are pulling your
team off the work
that matters.

Two people. Two to three hours on site. Then more time at the desk resolving dimensions that don’t quite agree — before a single design decision has been made. The cost of that shows up later: in the fee, in the programme, or in what gets missed on site.

01
A resourcing problem
Every hour a graduate or senior designer spends measuring a house is an hour not spent on design, or documentation. A manual measure-up on a typical A&A house ties up two people for half a day — before design has begun. That’s time coming off your project’s design budget before a single wall has been considered.
02
The reconciliation problem
Manual dimensions never perfectly add up. Someone experienced makes an educated guess. If that guess is wrong, the error propagates silently through the design until a builder finds it on site — where it becomes expensive, potentially a variation on your build contract, not just a line in the drawing.
03
The return visit
A missed ceiling height. A forgotten junction detail. A stair geometry that wasn’t quite right. Return visits are common, disruptive to the project programme, and require scheduling around the homeowner all over again. Every one of those pushes your completion date, and it’s usually your week that gets rearranged to fix it.

The technology

Leica BLK360 —
precision
in a single visit.

The Leica BLK360 is the industry-standard scanner for architectural as-built capture. In one site visit, it captures a complete spherical scan of the building — every room, wall, ceiling and floor junction, and external façade — to ±6mm accuracy. Multiple scan positions are registered together into a single, unified point cloud of the entire site: a millimetre-accurate 3D record you can walk through, measure, and reference at any stage of a project.

Delivered ready to work withThe point cloud is delivered as .RCP and .E57 files — open, industry-standard formats that load straight into Revit or ArchiCAD. No conversion, no cleanup. Cut plans at any height or sections at any angle, and trace directly over the point cloud to build an accurate BIM model — all in 3D, from the same file you started with.

Panorama imagery on KuulaFor heritage recordings or any project where a walkthrough adds value, the BLK360's panoramic imagery can be hosted as an interactive virtual tour on Kuula — a shareable way to walk clients through existing conditions remotely, or as reference for heritage report.

Why it matters

The scanner
is only half
the picture.

A point cloud is only as useful as the person who captures it. An operator who doesn’t understand buildings will miss the wall junction that matters, skip the ceiling step that changes everything, or fail to scan the roofline detail the heritage planner will ask about.

When RZ Scan attends your site, the person holding the scanner knows what your drawings need to show. Coverage decisions are made with your design process in mind — not just the equipment’s capabilities.

That’s the difference between data and useful data.
